CALCULATOR ============================================================

Inputs: - Basic Salary - Housing Allowance - Food Allowance - Transport

Unpaid Leave Deduction - Absence Deduction - Salary Advance or Loan

Earnings - Total Overtime - Gross Earnings - Total Deductions - Net

Starting formula: Gross Earnings = applicable earnings added once. Net
Salary = Gross Earnings − applicable deductions.

Treat this as a configurable calculation structure, not permission to
count every allowance as part of every legal wage calculation. Each wage
component must have clearly defined treatment for the relevant
calculation (ordinary wage, overtime, EOSB, leave pay, etc.).

============================================================ 8. OVERTIME
CALCULATOR ============================================================

Inputs: - Basic Monthly Salary - Applicable actual wage components -
Normal working hours and schedule - Overtime hours and minutes - Regular
working-day overtime - Weekly rest-day overtime - Public-holiday
overtime - Approved overtime categories - Selected calculation method
and rule version

Support both: - Decimal hours, e.g. 44.5 - Hours and minutes, e.g. 44
hours 30 minutes

44 hours 30 minutes equals 44.5 decimal hours, NOT 44.30 decimal hours.

Saudi Labor Law Article 107 generally describes overtime compensation as
the hourly wage plus an additional amount equal to 50% of the basic
hourly wage. Implement the currently applicable official rule and its
relevant definitions only after checking current official sources and
implementing regulations.

Requirements: - Distinguish ordinary hourly wage from basic hourly
wage. - Do not add 50% to the entire salary without legal
justification. - Do not invent a universal monthly-hours divisor. - Make
the hourly conversion basis visible and configurable only where legally
and contractually appropriate. - Avoid duplicate overtime payments. -
Explain the formula, wage components, divisor, time conversion, and
rounding. - Identify assumptions and uncertain cases. - Support company
payroll calculation separately from legal-rule calculation. - Flag
differences for review instead of automatically declaring a violation.

Private reference test case: Basic Salary SAR 2,050; Food Allowance SAR
200; overtime 44.5 hours; displayed overtime SAR 607.24.

Under one illustrative assumption—actual wage SAR 2,250 and hourly
conversion based on 30 days × 8 hours—the estimated overtime is
approximately SAR 607.74. Compare it with SAR 607.24 and flag the SAR
0.50 difference for investigation. This is a conditional test, not a
final legal determination.

CALCULATOR ============================================================

Inputs: - Employment start date - Employment end date - Basic wage -
Other wage components relevant to EOSB - Contract type - Reason for

Calculate service duration from dates, including relevant partial years,
months, and days, with a documented method.

Use current official Saudi legal sources and implementing regulations.
Relevant provisions may include Articles 84–87 and other applicable
provisions.

The general statutory structure under Article 84 includes half a month’s
wage for each of the first five years and one month’s wage for each
subsequent year, with proportional treatment for qualifying partial
years. Apply relevant resignation rules and exceptions based on the

Display: - Estimated full EOSB - Applicable estimated statutory
