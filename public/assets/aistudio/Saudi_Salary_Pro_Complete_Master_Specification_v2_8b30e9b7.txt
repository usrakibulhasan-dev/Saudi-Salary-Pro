PRODUCT VISION
Saudi Salary Pro is a modern, mobile-first, multilingual personal employment and salary management application for expatriate employees working in Saudi Arabia. The app owner, coworkers, and other employees should be able to open the same shareable public app link and register their own private accounts.

Every user manages their own records. The application is NOT an employer HR system and must not contain a shared employer employee directory, company-wide HR dashboard, or features that expose one user's private data to another user.

Primary users include expatriate employees who want to manage their work attendance, overtime, salary slips, leave, contracts, personal employment documents, benefits estimates, financial records, and career history.

============================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
============================================================

1. Personal ownership: every user owns and manages their own data.
2. Privacy by design: users must not be able to see other users' records.
3. Practical functionality: buttons, forms, calculators, exports, and data storage must actually work.
4. Transparent calculations: display formulas, assumptions, sources, and limitations.
5. Historical accuracy: never silently overwrite prior salary or employment history.
6. Multilingual from the start: do not bolt translations on at the end.
7. Mobile-first: optimize for phones while also supporting tablets and desktop.
8. Exportability: users must be able to take their own data with them.
9. Honest implementation: clearly disclose any incomplete integrations and configuration needs.
10. No fake integrations: do not simulate successful cloud backup, AI scanning, authentication, or notifications when they are not truly connected.

If backend, database, authentication, hosting, OAuth, or security rules need external setup, implement what is possible and list exact remaining steps. Do not call the application production-ready until the real configuration and security checks are completed.
